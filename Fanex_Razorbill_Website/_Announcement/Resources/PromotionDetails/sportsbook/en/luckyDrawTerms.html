The Lucky Draw promo of the Company will start from 12:00 noon GMT + 8 on 17 October 2012 and will continue until 8 th March 2013.

2. Who Can Join:

All Qualified Users of the Company are eligible to join the Lucky Draw. There will be a daily draw conducted by the Company during the Promo Period.

A "Qualified User" means a user of the Company website who (i) has registered a betting account with the Company; (ii) has logged into his/her account to access the Company website; (iii) has placed a single bet with a minimum amount of RM20.00 on any single event/market on the sportsbook, racing, number game and casino gaming products of the Company; and (iv) such bet is accepted and recorded into the system as one (1) bet slip or bet ticket during the Promo Period.

3. Disqualifications:

Employees of the Company, their affiliates and agents as well as their relatives up to second degree of consanguinity or affinity (e.g. parents, grandparents, spouses, children, and grandchildren) are disqualified from joining the Lucky Draw.

4. How to Join:

During Promo Period, a Qualified User will earn one (1) draw entry for every bet ticket with minimum bet of RM20.00 (or its currency equivalent) in any single event/market on the sportsbook, racing, number game and casino gaming products offered on the Company through its website. Any bet slip or bet ticket generated from the Poker gaming product of the Company, are not qualified to join the draw. The more draw entries earned by a Qualified User will give him/her more chances of winning.

Examples:

  • bet placed RM15.00 no entry
  • bet placed RM25.00 1 entry
  • bet placed RM50.00 1 entry
  • bet placed RM20.00 in one event and another bet placed RM15.00 in another event 1 entry
  • bet placed RM50.00 in one event and another bet placed RM100.00 in another event 2 entries

5. How Winning Entries are Drawn:

Winning entries will be drawn through an electronic draw/raffle system. There will be a daily draw per each day of the week. There will be one winner for the daily draws from Monday to Friday and two winners will be chosen for the daily draw on Saturday and Sunday.

All Qualified draw entries earned during any particular Business Day will be qualified to join the draw date scheduled for that particular Business Day. The draw dates for each Business Day is scheduled to be held two days after the date when the draw entry is earned in accordance with the table below:

Draw entries are only qualified to join the Draw scheduled for that particular Business Day in accordance in the above table.

The entries earned for any particular Business Day are not qualified and will not be included in the succeeding draws for the same week. A Qualified User can win on every daily draw.

For the purpose of identifying the entries to be included in a particular draw, a "week" means seven (7) consecutive business days starting from Monday to Sunday. A "business day" is a period of twenty four (24) consecutive hours starting from twelve o'clock noon (12:00nn) and ending on 12:00nn of the following day.
