Customer Info

Definition:

This function allows master to search and view the detailed information of agents, members which include Customer List, Statement and Log. To view information of your downline, key in the username.
Note:
  • A customer can be searched by username, first name or last name. Just input 2 characters, the list of most searched customers will be listed out.
  • To view all logs of that customer, click on the username, refers to View Log guideline to understand the details in this page.
  • To view list of downlines, click on Customer List.
  • To view the statement, click on Statement.
  • Customer List, Statement will be shown according to the customer that is searched in the text box.
