Agent Win/Loss Detail

Definition:

This report cover all information displayed in Agent Win Loss section.


figure 1.1

Term definition:

  • Turnover: The total amount of all member stakes.
  • Gross Comm: Maximum amount of money that master might receive from the upline as commission.
  • Member Win/Loss: Win/loss amount of member.
  • Member Comm: The amount of money that member receives from the agent as commission.
  • Member Total: Total member winloss amount including commission.
  • Agent Win/Loss: Win/loss amount of agent based on position taking.
  • Agent Comm: The amount of money that master gives agent as commission after deducting member commission.
  • Agent Total: Total agent winloss amount including commission.
  • Master Win/Loss: Win/loss amount of master based on position taking.
  • Master Comm: The amount of money that master receives from up-line as commission after deducting agent commission.
  • Master Total: Total master winloss amount including commission.
  • Company: The amount of money that master will pay to or receive from upline.

You can also click on the Old View link to view the Agent Win Loss Detail page in old view (figure 1.2).

The difference between the new and view one is the information of Agent Comm, Agent Total.


figure 1.2

Term definition:

  • Agent Comm: The amount of money that agent receives from upline as commission(has not deducted member commission yet).
  • Agent Total: The total amount of money that agent receives from or pays to master.
