Win/Loss By Bet type

Definition:

This report shows total member turnover, gross commission, master win/loss and the amount master has to pay to or receive from upline as company win/loss on each bet type in a period of time.


figure 1.1

Term definition:

  • Member Turnover: Total member stakes on each bet type.
  • Gross Comm: Maximum amount of money that master might receive from the up-line as commission on each bet type.
  • Agent Total: The amount of money that master receives from or pays to agent on each bet type.
  • Master Win/Loss: Amount of money that master wins/loses based on position taking on each bet type.
  • Master Comm: Amount of money that master receives from the up-line as commission after deducting agent.
  • Master Total: Total master win/loss amount including commission.
  • Company: The amount of money that master has to pay to or receive from up-line.
How to understand the report:
