Statement

Definition:

This is a brief report which sums all win/loss, commission, money transfer by each day and it helps master keep track of his/her daily income.


figure 1.1
How to understand the report:

From figure 1.1, master can have his/her summary report

For example:

  • Saturday 07/17/2010, the opening balance date and total balance up to that day of master was -42.86.
  • Tuesday 07/20/2010, master earned 30 based on position taking then his/her balance = -42.86 + 30 = -12.86.
  • Tuesday 07/20/2010, master lost 0.03 as commission after gives agent commission then his/her balance = -12.86 - 0.03 = -12.89.
  • Wednesday 07/21/2010, master lost 2.4 based on position taking then his/her balance = -12.89 - 2.4 = -15.29.
  • Wednesday 07/21/2010, master lost 0.02 as commission after giving agent commission then his/her balance = -15.29 - 0.02 = -15.31.
  • Thursday 07/22/2010, master earned 0.01 as commission after gives agent commission then his/her balance = -15.31 + 0.01 = -15.31.
  • Thursday 07/22/2010, master earned 1.5 based on position taking then his/her balance = -15.31 + 1.5 = -13.81.
  • Friday 07/23/2010, master transferred 34.27 to agent1 then his/her balance = -13.81 - 34.27 = -48.08.
  • Friday 07/23/2010, master received 48.08 from supertest1 then his/her balance = -48.08 + 48.08 = 0.

REMARK: display operations at correlative date.
