Odd/Even + 1x2

Definition:

Show master’s win/loss based on position taking for tickets which have bet type: Odd/Even, 1x2 for 1st Half or Full Time (figure 1.1).


figure 1.1

  • In the figure above, members bet on 2 leagues:
    • JAPAN J-LEAGUE DIVISION 1
    • KOREAN NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP
  • With JAPAN J-LEAGUE DIVISION 1, member1 placed two bets on match Montedio Yamagata -vs- Kawasaki Frontale and Omiya Ardija -vs- Yokohama Marinos with the following details:
    • 1st bet: stake = 70, odds = 3.52, bet type: 1x2, Full Time, choice: FT.1, Master PT = 30% --> Master's earning based on PT = 70*30% = 21
    • 2nd bet: stake = 60, odds = 2.7, bet type: 1x2, 1st Half, choice: HT.2, Master PT = 30% --> Master's earning based on PT = 60*30% = 18
  • With KOREAN NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP, member1 placed a bet on match Gimhae FC (N) -vs- Incheon Korail the following details:
    • 1st bet: stake = 80, odds = 0.96, bet type: Odd/Even, Full Time, bet Odd, Master PT = 30% --> Master's earning based on PT = 80*30% = 24

Forecast function

This function helps master figure out win/loss amount based on Home score, Away score. To view the winlost forecast, click “+” sign.


figure 1.2

  • Full Time 1x2: In this example, member1 placed a bet on match Montedio Yamagata -vs- Kawasaki Frontale, stake= 70, choice: FT.1, odds= 3.52, bettype=FullTime 1x2, master position taking = 30%.


    figure 1.3

    This is 1x2 Forecast of Full Time result and details as below:
    • 1: 53 --> If Home team win then Master will lose: 70*30%*(3.52 - 1) = 53.
    • x: 21 --> If the result is draw then Master will win: 70*30% = 21.
    • 2: 21 --> If Away team win, then Master will win: 70*30% = 21.

  • 1st Half 1x2: In this example, member1 placed a bet on match Omiya Ardija -vs- Yokohama Marinos, stake= 60,choice: HT.2, odds= 2.7, bettype=1st Half 1x2, master position taking = 30%.

    This is 1x2 Forecast of First Half result and details as below:

    • x: 18 --> If the result is draw then Master will win: 60*30% = 18.
    • 2: 31 --> If Away team win then Master will lose: 60*30%*(2.7 - 1) = 31.
