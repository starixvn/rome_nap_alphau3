Half Time/Full Time

Definition:

Show master's win/loss amount based on Half Time, Full Time result.


figure 1.1

  • H: Home           A: Away            D: Draw
  • HA means:
    • First Half: Home team will win.
    • Full Time: Away team will win.
    • 1 GIF Sundsvall -vs- Hammarby
      • Final result: HH --> master will lose 22.2.
      • Final result: DH --> master will lose 55.5.
      • Final result: others --> master will win 39.
    • 2 Syrianska FC -vs- Osters IF
      • Final result: HD --> master will lose 384.
      • Final result: others --> master will win 24.
