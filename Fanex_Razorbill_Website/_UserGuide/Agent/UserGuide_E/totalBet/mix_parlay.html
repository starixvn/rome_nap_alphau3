Mix Parlay

Definition:

Show agent’s win/loss based on position taking for tickets which have bet type: Mix Parlay.


Mix Parlay_01
For Mix Parlay bet type, you must parlay a minimum of 3 matches on a single sport or different sports.To view the detail of each Mix Parlay bet, just click "MixParlay" under Choice column.


Mix Parlay_02
Notes:
  • For Mix Parlay tickets that players bet on different sports or any single sport except Soccer, then Mix Sports Parlay setting will be applied.
  • For Mix Parlay tickets that players bet on Soccer only, refer to Soccer Mix Parlay setting.
  • If player loses any match among these matches, the Mix Parlay ticket will be treated as Loss.
  • If any match in which player loses half, odds of that match = 0.5
  • If any match in which player wins half, odds of that match = [ (odds -1) /2 ] + 1
