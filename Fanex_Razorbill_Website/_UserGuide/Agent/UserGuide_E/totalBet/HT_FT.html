Half Time/Full Time

Definition:

Show agent's win/loss amount based on Half Time , Full Time result.


Half Time/Full Time

  • H: Home           A: Away            D: Draw
  • HA means:
    • First Half: Home team will win
    • Full Time: Away team will win
  • 1 Melbourne Victory -vs- Wellington Phoenix FC
    • Final result: HH --> Agent will lose 15
    • Final result: DH --> Agent will lose 115
    • Final result: others --> Agent will win 100
  • 2 Queensland Roar -vs- Perth Glory
    • Final result: HA --> Agent will lose 3000
    • Final result: others --> Agent will win 100
  • 3 Sydney FC -vs- Newscattle Jets
    • Final result: DD --> Agent will lose 200
    • Final result: others --> Agent will win 100
