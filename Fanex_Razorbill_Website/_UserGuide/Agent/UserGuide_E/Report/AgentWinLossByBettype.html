Win/Loss By Bet type

Definition:

This report shows total member turnover, member win/loss, agent win/loss and the amount agent has to pay to or receive from upline as company win/loss on each bet type in a period of time.


figure 5.1

Term definition:

  • Turnover: total member stakes on each bet type.
  • Member Win/Loss: win/loss amount of members on each bet type.
  • Member Comm: amount of money that members received from the agent as commission.
  • Member Total: total members win/loss amount including commission.
  • Agent Win/Loss: amount of money that agent wins/loses based on position taking.
  • Agent Comm: amount of money that agent receives from the upline as commission after deducting member commission.
  • Agent total: total agent win/loss amount including commission.
  • Company: the amount of money that agent has to pay to or receive from upline.
How to understand the report:
