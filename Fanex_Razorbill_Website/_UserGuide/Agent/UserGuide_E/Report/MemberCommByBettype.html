Commission by Bet type

Definition:

This is to inform agent the commission they will have after deducting member commission. The report is classified by each member on each particular bet type (Figure 6.1).

How to understand the report:

From the figure 6.1 above, we can see that agent lost total 1.47 as commission after deducting member commission and below is the detailed information.

  • Bet type Over/Under: agent has to take 0.27 from his pocket to pay member commission.
