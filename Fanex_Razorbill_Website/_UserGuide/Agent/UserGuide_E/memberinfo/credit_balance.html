Credit balance

Definition:

The function is to show all necessary information regarding member credit and balance such as:
  • Status: open, closed or suspended.
  • Credit: total member credit given by agent.
  • Balance: total win/loss up to now.
  • Yesterday balance: total win/loss up to yesterday.
  • Bet credit: total available member credit to place bet.
  • Outstanding: total betting amount on running tickets.
  • Member turnover: total betting amount on both running and settled tickets.
Futhermore, this function also allows agent with these following sub-function:
  • Increase or decrease member given credit by clicking Credit amount.
  • Make a tranfer by clicking Yesterday Balance amount.
  • View bet list, daily statement by clicking Outstanding amount.
