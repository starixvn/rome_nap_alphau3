Configure Live Casino Win Limit
How to configure “Win Limit” for member?
Step 1
Go to “Customer List”, click on “Edit”>”Live Casino”
Step 2
Under “Member Win Limit”, use the drop down box to choose the desired “Max Win Multiplier” for win limit
- “Maximum win” will be equal to “member credit limit” multiply with “Max win multiplier”
- Example: Member credit = 1,000; Max win multiplier = 3X
- Member maximum win will be 1,000 X 3 = 3,000
- Member will be unable to place bet once his winning greater than or equals to 3,000.
Remark: Member winning is possible to exceed “Maximum win” based his last bet amount.
Step 3
Ticked on “Daily auto reset limit”
- If ticked, it will daily reset member win limit.
- If not ticked, member win limit will only reset after transfer
Step 4
Click to view Current Win Limit

- it will show the current win limit percentage of member
Step 5
How to view “Current member win limit” in “Credit/Balance” Page
  • Go to “Credit/Balance” Page
  • Right click on “Title bar” and tick “LC Win %”
  • New column will show for “LC Win %”
